## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-fintech atmosphere crafted specifically for Indonesian MSMEs (*UMKM*) and angel/impact investors. The aesthetic fuses the grounded prestige of private wealth institutions with the accessible, tactile warmth required for entrepreneurs of diverse digital literacy levels across the archipelago.

### Brand Personality & Core Tenets
- **Empowering & Dignified:** Eliminates predatory lending aesthetics. Replaces aggressive microfinance cues with measured, editorial prestige that respects both the seasoned investor and the family-owned business owner.
- **Rooted Stability:** Conveys institutional safety, legal compliance (*OJK*-grade trust cues), and patient capital through deep botanical forest greens, bone-tinted parchment canvases, and crisp financial typography.
- **Radically Approachable:** Accommodates high-frequency mobile usage, variable screen brightness under tropical daylight, and one-handed thumb navigation through generous touch volumes and friction-free conversational forms.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial Modernism meets Tactile FinTech:** Generous white/bone margins, sharp typographic hierarchy, and warm paper-like backdrops are paired with tactile, thumb-friendly touch surfaces, delicate single-pixel hairline borders, and atmospheric elevation to produce confidence without intimidating grass-roots operators.

## Colors

The color system pairs deep botanical pigments with sun-bleached organic backdrops to create a secure, paper-grounded financial interface.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#F3EEE3`):** Bone White serves as the default application canvas, reducing optical glare outdoors and evoking financial journals and certificates.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp, untinted white elevates interactive cards and sheets above the bone substrate, producing effortless visual layering without heavy drop shadows.
- **Brand Primary (`#0F2419`):** Deep Forest Green delivers maximum contrast for primary calls-to-action, bottom sheets, navigation headers, and authoritative legal terms.
- **Brand Accent (`#2D6A4F`):** Rich Pine Green designates active states, focused form controls, key metric tags, and secondary action surfaces.
- **Brand Tint / Highlight (`#8FBFA0`):** Soft Mint provides subtle backdrops for category chips, progress bars, and soft accents.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Text Primary (`#16241D`):** Deep Charcoal Green ensures optimal contrast against white and bone grounds while maintaining organic tonal harmony with the primary green.
- **Text Secondary / Muted (`#4A5B52`):** Olive Charcoal provides soft readability for timestamps, contextual balance sheets, field labels, and explanatory notes.
- **Border / Divider Hairline (`#E2DDD2`):** Low-contrast warm gray creates subtle division without cluttering compact mobile viewports.

### System & Status Accents
System states use unmuted functional colors distinct from brand greens to prevent ambiguity during financial transactions:
- **Verified / Success (`#10B981`):** Electric Emerald confirms business verification (*Terverifikasi*), escrow deposit confirmations, and matched funding rounds.
- **Pending / In Review (`#F59E0B`):** Warm Amber Yellow denotes pending due diligence (*Sedang Ditinjau*), waiting for investor sign-off, or incomplete KYC documents.
- **Alert / Destructive (`#EF4444`):** Crimson Red signals rejected proposals, payment timeouts, and high-risk metric disclosures.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its tall x-height, open apertures, and geometric clarity across varying screen qualities and daylight environments.

### Typographic Rules
- **Financial Numeric Presentation:** All currency figures (IDR / *Rupiah*) must be rendered with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column alignment across balance sheets, projection charts, and revenue ledgers.
- **Headline Scannability:** Headlines use tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and confident weights (`700` and `800`) to evoke modern business editorial publications.
- **Language Consideration:** Indonesian text requires room for longer compounds (*Pertanggungjawaban*, *Pendanaan Bersama*). Line heights for body copy are kept loose (`1.45` to `1.5`) to prevent multiline sentence collisions in dense microcopy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around progressive web app constraints, maximizing ergonomic reachability for single-thumb execution on 360px–430px mobile viewports.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` (16px) margins and `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Essential controls and high-frequency CTAs sit in a sticky bottom bar within the primary thumb reach zone.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` (24px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at `75rem` (1200px) centered width, with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Two-pane layout splits matching filters/diligence criteria on the left and investment opportunities on the right.

### Ergonomic Principles
- **Touch Target Integrity:** Every interactive control—navigation tabs, accordion triggers, chips, and buttons—strictly adheres to a minimum tap height and width of `3rem` (48px).
- **Sticky Action Shelf:** Floating bottom sheets on mobile reserve `4.5rem` height at the bottom to account for browser chrome, system home indicators, and rapid one-tap commitment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient depth: crisp surfaces elevate above the bone canvas with diffuse, tinted shadows rather than sterile gray tones.

### Layer Tiers
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** `#F3EEE3` (Bone White). Flat background with zero shadow.
- **Standard Card Surface (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-delicate outline (`1px solid rgba(22, 36, 29, 0.06)`) and a warm ambient drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.05), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.03);`
- **Floating Controls & Dropdowns (Level 2):** Sticky action bars, filter trays, and dropdown cards:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.08), 0 16px 28px -6px rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.06);`
- **Modals & Match Overlays (Level 3):** Verification dialogs, loan agreements, and investor term sheets:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.16), 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.12);`
  Protected by a dimming backdrop layer using `rgba(15, 36, 25, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines rounded contours with structured internal layouts, balancing a warm, friendly touch with structural rigor.

### Rounding Matrix
- **Cards & Match Panels:** Use `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) to define self-contained business profiles and investment units.
- **Buttons & Text Fields:** Use `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to communicate tappability and high tactile quality.
- **Chips, Badges, & Financial Indicators:** Use `rounded-full` (9999px) for pill tags (e.g., sector tags like *F&B*, *Kriya*, *Agrikultur* and verification badges).
- **Thumb Controls & Checkboxes:** Use `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px) to avoid clinical sharp edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Commitment & Matching):** Background `#0F2419`, text `#FFFFFF`, height `52px` (mobile) / `48px` (desktop), border-radius `12px`. Hover shifts to `#2D6A4F`. Active state downscales subtly to `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Action (Inquiry & Profiling):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F2419`, border `1.5px solid #0F2419`, height `52px`/`48px`.
- **Tertiary / Soft Ghost:** Background `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08)`, text `#2D6A4F`, no border.
- **Disabled State:** Background `#E2DDD2`, text `#8B9890`, cursor `not-allowed`.

### Cards (MSME Profile & Deal Feed)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `16px`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(22, 36, 29, 0.06)`. Internal padding `20px`.
- Top row highlights the business category pill, location badge (*e.g., Bandung, Jawa Barat*), and verified shield icon.
- Central numeric grid displays *Target Modal* (Target Capital), *Tenor* (Term length), and *Estimasi ROI* in bold, tabular figures.
- Bottom actions provide a side-by-side arrangement: secondary icon button for bookmarking and a primary button for proposal review (*Pelajari Usaha*).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact pills with `4px 10px` padding, font weight `700`, text size `11px`, letter-spacing `0.04em`.
  - *Terverifikasi (Verified):* Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#0D7A55`, leading checkmark icon.
  - *Dalam Kurasi (Pending):* Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#9A6304`.
  - *Belum Lengkap (Alert):* Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#B91C1C`.
- **Filter Chips:** Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2DDD2`, border-radius `9999px`. Selected state switches to background `#0F2419` with text `#FFFFFF`.

### Input Fields
- Minimum touch container height `52px` with internal padding `14px 16px`.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D8D2C5`, text `#16241D`, placeholder `#8B9890`.
- Focused state features `border-color: #2D6A4F` with a soft outline `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.15)`.
- Numeric investment inputs feature a permanently pinned left prefix label `Rp` in bold Olive Charcoal.
- Reassuring microcopy sits directly below each input (*e.g., "Data usaha Anda dilindungi enkripsi standar perbankan"*).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `22px x 22px` positioned inside a container with at least `48px` tap allowance.
- Checkbox uses `border-radius: 6px`, border `2px solid #4A5B52`. When checked, background fills with `#0F2419` containing a clean white SVG checkmark.
- Radio uses concentric circles with a `#0F2419` center dot.

### Lists & Information Rows
- Diligence checklists, transaction histories, and legal disclosure items use divided rows with `1px solid #E2DDD2`.
- Left-aligned title and explanatory subtext; right-aligned bold financial values or status indicators with forward-facing chevron icons (`16px`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Trust Banner / Verification Bar:** Prominent top-of-card panel featuring the verified emblem, OJK compliance advisory notice, and date of last financial audit.
- **Funding Metric Gauge:** Custom horizontal track indicator with track `#E2DDD2`, fill `#2D6A4F`, height `8px`, and border-radius `9999px`, accompanied by percentage funded (*e.g., 78% Terkumpul*) and days remaining.
